An AI assistant that can read a folder (for example Claude Cowork) can draft your journals, rules and authors from your own research notes. Copy the setup prompt, give it to the assistant together with access to your notes folder, then import the profile file it writes. You see a preview first, and importing only adds to your settings. Your notes are read by the assistant you choose; Research Radar never reads them. Copy setup prompt Import profile… Research interests A few sentences about your research, in plain language. Used by the optional AI ranking below. Asks an AI to rate each new article 0–3 against your research interests (above). The rating is shown next to the keyword score; an article rated 2 or 3 goes to Radar/Possible even if no rule matched. Nothing is ever hidden. Only titles, abstracts and your interests paragraph are sent, never anything from your library. If the AI can't be reached (e.g. off the VPN), Research Radar quietly uses the keyword scores alone. With an ORCID iD, matching is exact and namesakes are ignored; without one, family name plus first initial is used.
