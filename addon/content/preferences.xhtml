Articles are fetched from Crossref by ISSN; an RSS feed URL is optional and mainly adds abstracts that some publishers don't send to Crossref. Search any journal Search Crossref Words are matched in any form (decline, declining) and in British or American spelling. Use "quotes" for phrases, AND / OR / NOT (capitals), brackets, and * for word families: histor* matches history and historical. Title matches count 3× abstract matches by default. With an ORCID iD, matching is exact and namesakes are ignored; without one, family name plus first initial is used.
